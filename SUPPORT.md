# Support

- **Connecting a client:** [opendiavgeia.com/connect](https://opendiavgeia.com/connect) and the [mcp README](https://github.com/opendiavgeia/mcp#connect).
- **A wrong figure, a name the search can't find, a client that won't connect:** open an issue in [opendiavgeia/mcp](https://github.com/opendiavgeia/mcp/issues), with the page address or the question you asked.
- **Removing a person's name, or anything you'd rather not post in public:** contact@opendiavgeia.com.

Decisions are published by the public bodies themselves on [diavgeia.gov.gr](https://diavgeia.gov.gr). An error in a decision's own text can only be corrected by the body that posted it.
