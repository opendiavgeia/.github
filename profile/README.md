# opendiavgeia

[opendiavgeia.com](https://opendiavgeia.com) is an independent search over [Diavgeia](https://diavgeia.gov.gr), where Greek public bodies post their decisions. It adds up what each ministry, municipality, region and hospital paid, and to whom, by year and by tax number (ΑΦΜ), and links every figure to the decision it came from. It also lists the open public tenders from [ΚΗΜΔΗΣ](https://cerpp.eprocurement.gov.gr) and [TED](https://ted.europa.eu), each with what its buyer paid for similar work before.

Το opendiavgeia.com είναι μια ανεξάρτητη αναζήτηση στη Διαύγεια: τι πλήρωσε κάθε φορέας, σε ποιον και πότε, με σύνδεσμο στην απόφαση από την οποία προκύπτει κάθε ποσό. Δείχνει και τους ανοιχτούς διαγωνισμούς από το ΚΗΜΔΗΣ και το TED.

- **Site:** [opendiavgeia.com](https://opendiavgeia.com)
- **Open tenders:** [opendiavgeia.com/tenders](https://opendiavgeia.com/tenders), by sector, region, budget and deadline
- **Ask AI:** [opendiavgeia.com/ask](https://opendiavgeia.com/ask), questions in Greek or English
- **MCP server:** `https://opendiavgeia.com/mcp`, for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and VS Code ([setup](https://github.com/opendiavgeia/mcp))
- **For AI assistants:** [opendiavgeia.com/llms.txt](https://opendiavgeia.com/llms.txt)
- **Contact:** contact@opendiavgeia.com

### Data

Tenders come from ΚΗΜΔΗΣ and TED. Decisions come from Diavgeia's open API and, back to 2010, from the [glossAPI/diavgeia](https://huggingface.co/datasets/glossAPI/diavgeia) archive (CC BY 4.0). [How amounts are counted](https://opendiavgeia.com/about).

opendiavgeia is not affiliated with diavgeia.gov.gr or any public authority.
